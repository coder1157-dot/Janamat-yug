About Janamat Yug

An independent newsroom built for the internet age, reporting India's story with speed, accuracy and heart.

Our Story

Journalism for every reader, in every corner of India

Janamat Yug — "the era of public opinion" — was founded on a simple belief: that reliable news should be free, fast and written in the language people actually speak. From city newsrooms to district correspondents, our team chases stories that matter to ordinary readers, not just headlines.

Today we cover national affairs, business, sport, entertainment and technology, with a growing network of contributors across states — verifying every claim before it reaches your screen.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate, unbiased and timely news to every Indian household — cutting through noise with verified reporting, plain language, and respect for the reader's time and trust.

Our Vision

A more informed India, where every citizen — from metro cities to small towns — has equal access to the facts that shape their lives, delivered without spin, sensationalism or paywalls.
